The Battlefield (Problem Statements)

Choose your mission from our 10 Marvel-inspired IoT challenges:

Smart Campus Management

Energy optimization and real-time infrastructure response, inspired by Stark Tower.

Ambulance Green Corridor

IoT systems to shift traffic lights and clear paths instantly for ambulances.

Remote Patient Monitoring

Real-time emergency detection and monitoring for hospital systems.

Crowd Safety & Stampede Risk

Monitor crowd density and predict surges before they escalate into crises.

Wildlife Conservation

Anti-poaching network using drones and camera processing for forest monitoring.

Child Nutrition Monitoring

Tracking school meal details, BMI, and flag irregular growth patterns.

Disaster Response

Digital command center mapping affected zones via drones and satellites.

Women Safety System

SOS triggers sharing live location and context-aware automatic alerts.

Ferry Service Safety

Ensuring waterway safety and collision prevention during storm conditions.

Smart Irrigation

IoT monitoring of soil moisture and environmental conditions for optimized water use.
