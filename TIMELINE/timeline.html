JOURNEY OF IOTA

From a small group of hardware enthusiasts to the official IoT Applications Club of Jadavpur University. Here's our story of innovation and growth.

August 2023

The Genesis

IOTA Club was officially conceptualized and founded by a group of passionate students at Jadavpur University Main Campus, aiming to create a dedicated space for IoT and hardware innovation.

September 2023

Recruitment Drive

Conducted the first official recruitment drive, onboarding 25+ talented members across hardware, software, and design domains.

October 2023

First Workshop: 'IoT Basics'

Launched our first hands-on workshop, introducing students to ESP32, sensors, and cloud integration. Over 100 students participated in the session.

December 2023

Project 'Ishara' Phase 1

Kicked off our flagship project 'Ishara' - a smart sign-language translator, utilizing advanced sensor integration and AI-based gesture recognition.

February 2024

Hackathon Success

IOTA teams participated and secured top positions in several inter-university hackathons, showcasing projects like 'VoxVision' and the IoT-based 'Road Anomaly Detector'.

April 2024

'Code-To-Flight' Bootcamp

Organized a massive multi-day drone bootcamp, covering everything from flight dynamics to autonomous navigation using Pixhawk and Raspberry Pi.

January 2025

Industry Collaborations

Established partnerships with local hardware startups and tech companies to facilitate internships and industrial project opportunities for our members.

Innovating the Future with IoT. Join us in building smart solutions for a better tomorrow.
