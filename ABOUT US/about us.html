WE ARE IOTA

The IoT Applications Club at Jadavpur University is a collective of visionary engineers dedicated to bridging the gap between the digital and physical worlds.

Founded in 2023, we explore the frontiers of Robotics, Sensors, and Communication systems, fueled by a passion for pure innovation.

Our Mission

To architect a collaborative sanctuary where the brightest minds at Jadavpur University converge to explore, dismantle, and rebuild the future of connected technology. We foster a culture of radical technical excellence and professional growth.

Our Vision

To become the epicenter of IoT research and product development, setting new benchmarks for university-led hardware innovation.

What We Do

Advanced R&D

We push microcontrollers to their absolute limits, developing low-latency sensor networks and smart systems that solve local challenges.

Knowledge Fusion

Regular deep-dive workshops on Embedded C, PCB design, and RTOS, ensuring our members are industry-ready from day one.

Robotics & AI

Merging mechanical precision with intelligent algorithms to create autonomous systems that can perceive and interact with the real world.
