ABOUT US

The IoT Applications Club at Jadavpur University (IOTA JU), founded in 2023, is driven by a compelling mission: to unite enthusiastic engineering students dedicated to pioneering research and development within the dynamic realm of the Internet of Things (IoT).

Our expansive focus encompasses exciting fields such as Robotics, Sensors, Communication Systems, and Networking, while also delving into AI/ML, Cloud Computing, and Data Science.

Our Mission

To create a collaborative environment where students can explore, learn, and innovate in the field of Internet of Things, fostering technical excellence and professional growth.

Our Vision

To be the leading IoT community at Jadavpur University, driving innovation and preparing students for the future of connected technology.

What We Do

Research & Development

Pioneering research in IoT technologies, sensors, and connected systems.

Workshops & Seminars

Regular technical workshops and seminars to enhance practical skills.

Projects & Innovation

Hands-on projects covering robotics, AI/ML, and cloud computing.

Community Building

Building a strong network of IoT enthusiasts and professionals.

Innovating the Future with IoT. Join us in building smart solutions for a better tomorrow.
